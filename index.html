01Who we are

A bridge between India and the world's decision-makers.

India's global engagement has never mattered more. As one of the world's fastest-growing major economies and an increasingly central voice in global affairs, India is where strategy, diplomacy and commerce converge.

ACE Consulting Group helps organisations navigate this landscape with clarity and confidence. We combine an understanding of foreign policy and geopolitics with practical, on-the-ground stakeholder engagement, so that our clients can build relationships, enter markets and grow across borders.

Whether you are a foreign government deepening ties with India, a global company seeking market entry, or an Indian institution going international, we help you connect with the right people, move with purpose, and exchange value that endures.

  • /01

    Global perspective

    We read policy, markets and geopolitics together, because our clients' challenges never sit in just one of them.

  • /02

    Integrity & discretion

    We engage every stakeholder transparently and handle client matters with discretion.

  • /03

    Outcome driven

    Introductions alone aren't the goal. We measure success in partnerships signed, markets opened and dialogues sustained.

03Our approach

Connect. Move. Exchange.

Three principles guide every engagement, from a single strategic dialogue to a multi-year market entry programme.

Connect

We map the stakeholder landscape across government, industry, academia and civil society, and help you engage the right stakeholders in a transparent, well-prepared way.

Move

We turn introductions into momentum with clear strategy, well-prepared engagement and steady follow-through that keeps initiatives moving across borders.

Exchange

We build partnerships where ideas, investment, talent and culture flow both ways, creating value for every side of the table.
