Who We Are

A bridge between India and the world's decision-makers.

India's global engagement has never mattered more. As one of the world's fastest-growing major economies and an increasingly central voice in global affairs, India is where strategy, diplomacy and commerce converge.

ACE Consulting Group helps organisations navigate this landscape with clarity and confidence. We combine an understanding of foreign policy and geopolitics with practical, on-the-ground stakeholder engagement, so that our clients can build relationships, enter markets and shape outcomes across borders.

Whether you are a foreign government deepening ties with India, a global company seeking market entry, or an Indian institution going international, we help you connect with the right people, move with purpose, and exchange value that endures.

Global perspective

We read policy, markets and geopolitics together, because our clients' challenges never sit in just one of them.

Discretion & trust

Diplomatic work runs on confidence. We handle every mandate with the discretion it deserves.

Outcome driven

Access alone isn't the goal. We measure success in partnerships signed, markets opened and dialogues sustained.

Our Approach

Connect. Move. Exchange.

Three principles guide every engagement, from a single strategic dialogue to a multi-year market entry programme.

I

Connect

We map the stakeholder landscape across government, industry, academia and civil society, and open doors to the people who shape outcomes.

II

Move

We turn access into momentum with clear strategy, well-prepared engagement and steady follow-through that keeps initiatives moving across borders.

III

Exchange

We build partnerships where ideas, investment, talent and culture flow both ways, creating value for every side of the table.

    Understand

    We start with your objectives, constraints and the wider political and economic context.

  2. 02

    Map

    We identify the stakeholders, decision pathways, risks and openings that matter.

    Engage

    Sustain

    We help turn early wins into lasting partnerships, with ongoing advisory and support.
